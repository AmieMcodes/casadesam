FOUNDING BOARD · CASA DE SAM

A small beginning. A lasting purpose.

We’re looking for Paraguayan and American people who want to help protect a simple promise: a lifelong home where adults with significant support needs belong, without having to earn their place.

01

First, help lay the foundations.

Between now and formation, we expect a modest planning commitment: read the vision, offer thoughtful feedback, join occasional conversations, and contribute relevant experience or connections. This is an invitation to explore, not a request to take on an undefined job.

Our goal is to establish the nonprofit in Paraguay and a Friends of organization in the United States in February–March 2027. This is a planning target, subject to legal guidance and the formation process—not a guaranteed completion date.

Light planning does not mean light responsibility.

Formal board service carries real governance and legal duties. Roles, terms, responsibilities, and the appropriate organization would be discussed before any appointment, with professional guidance.

Board service is unpaid. Expressing interest is not a commitment to join, donate, or raise funds.

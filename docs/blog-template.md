\# About Casa de SAM



Use the standard factual description unless there is a reason to adjust it:



Casa de SAM is a developing vision for a lifelong residential community in Paraguay for adults with developmental disabilities who need ongoing support. We believe joyful living is inherently valuable, and our promise is that once a resident is fully accepted, Casa de SAM will care for them for life. The project is currently in its planning and organisational stage, with a long-term goal of opening in 2035. Casa de SAM is not yet a registered nonprofit and is not currently accepting donations.



Do not hard-code CTA language here. CTA components are managed separately.

\# Final Editorial Review



Before converting the article to HTML, confirm:



\- \[ ] The search question is answered near the beginning.

\- \[ ] The article provides more than a basic definition.

\- \[ ] The article contains substantial advice and practical detail.

\- \[ ] Important trade-offs are explained.

\- \[ ] The article includes a checklist or reusable question set.

\- \[ ] The article includes realistic steps the reader can take this week.

\- \[ ] Resources are trustworthy and individually explained.

\- \[ ] Location-specific limitations are clear.

\- \[ ] Personal experience supports rather than replaces evidence.

\- \[ ] Casa de SAM is presented factually rather than as the answer to every problem.

\- \[ ] The article does not manipulate fear.

\- \[ ] The article respects disabled adults’ dignity, choices, communication, and adulthood.

\- \[ ] The disclaimer matches the article.

\- \[ ] Internal links are genuinely useful.

\- \[ ] The article passes the Casa de SAM Test.
